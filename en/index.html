The mission

The biggest challenge on the streets of Madrid

You've found a SmartWatch that pulls you into an adventure with different screens where you'll have to decode and beat a series of challenges to move forward.

The route is set at real Madrid locations: Retiro Park, the Prado Museum, the Temple of Debod, Puerta del Sol and the Royal Palace. Each challenge you beat brings you one step closer to defusing the bomb — but you only get one shot to pull it off.

Frequently asked questions

Before you accept the mission

Do I need to be physically in Madrid to play?

The game is set at real Madrid locations such as Retiro Park, the Prado Museum, the Temple of Debod, Sol and the Royal Palace, and is played directly from your own device.

Can I try again if I fail?

According to the app itself, you only get one attempt to defuse the bomb, so it's worth reading each clue carefully before answering.

Is it free?

The app contains ads and also offers optional in-game purchases.

What age is it suitable for?

Google Play's content rating lists it for ages 3 and up, though the difficulty of the puzzles is aimed at teens and adults.
